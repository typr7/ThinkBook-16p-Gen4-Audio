## Firmware, SDK and other dependencies

OEM/Cirrus firmware, tuning and generated byte arrays are excluded. `Resources/OEM-firmware.json` identifies the exact files required for this board; hashes do not establish redistribution permission. Obtain the files with the matching OEM Windows audio driver and review the accompanying terms. Locally generated kexts embed these files and are not automatically covered for redistribution by any source license here.

MacKernelSDK is fetched separately at the pinned commit in `provenance.json` and remains under its upstream component licenses. AppleALC and Lilu are external runtime dependencies and are not bundled. Machine calibration must come from the target machine, not from this repository.
